Keep a fixed 10×10 character screen, cursor row and column, and the insert-mode flag. Read each data line character by character while ignoring its newline; `getchar` preserves ordinary spaces in the line.

On `^`, consume the command and, for a coordinate command, one additional digit. Only `^^` and ordinary characters enter the write path. In insert mode, shift the row suffix from right to left before writing; overwrite mode changes one cell. Clamp the cursor at column 9. Clearing the screen keeps the cursor, while erase-to-end starts at its current column.
