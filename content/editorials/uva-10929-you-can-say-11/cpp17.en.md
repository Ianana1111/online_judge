The number remains a string and is compared exactly with the sentinel `"0"`. A fresh remainder starts at zero for every case.

Each character becomes its digit by subtracting `'0'`; multiplication, addition, and modulo remain tiny integers. No floating-point or string-to-integer conversion is used.

The original string is untouched for output. The conditional inserts either the affirmative or negative fixed sentence, including its final period.
