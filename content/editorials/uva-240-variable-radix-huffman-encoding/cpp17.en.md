`add` stores a persistent node and a queue item pointing to its ID. The comparator selects smallest weight then letter; real indices precede dummy indices beginning at 26.

Children remain in pop order, so traversal edge index is its radix digit. Leaves write codes only for real letters. Weighted and total sums exclude dummies, and `fixed_ratio` performs exact nearest two-decimal rounding. Each set prints prescribed indentation and a trailing blank line.
