`signature` receives its argument by value, so sorting cannot modify the original stored word. Parallel `words` and `keys` vectors share indices, allowing a signature match to return the exact original text. `answer` is recreated for every query, resetting numbering.

`setw(3)` applies only to the next integer and creates the required number field before `) `. A blank line is printed only between datasets. Formatted extraction skips separator whitespace, and the `END` check occurs before any header or result is emitted.
