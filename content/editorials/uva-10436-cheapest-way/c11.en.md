With fewer than 20 stations, names live in a fixed `name[20][64]` array, and `find_station` maps a name to its index with a linear scan and `strcmp`. That is at most 19 comparisons and simpler than writing a hash table. Strings are read with `%63s` so they cannot overflow the buffer.

The `road` matrix is reset to `INF` for each map, and parallel roads keep only the shorter length. Each query runs an O(n²) Dijkstra with the arrays `cost`, `prev` and `done`; the start's cost is its own fee, and moving from x to y costs 2 × distance plus y's fee.

The route is stored in `path` by following `prev` from the destination, then printed in reverse. The fare is computed in whole cents with rounding and printed with `%lld.%02lld`, where `%02lld` makes 5 cents print as `05`. The blank line between maps is printed only when m > 1.
