The `first` argument chooses which operator is absorbed within a group. When addition is first, completed groups are multiplied and the answer starts at one; when multiplication is first, groups are added and the answer starts at zero.

Encountering the other operator commits the current group and begins the next value. After the operator loop, one uncommitted group remains and is combined in the return expression. `stringstream` reads full one- or two-digit integers and optional spaces. There is always one more value than operator.
