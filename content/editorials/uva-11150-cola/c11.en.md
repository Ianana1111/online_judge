Track empty bottles rather than simulating every exchange. Trading three empties for one drink returns one empty after drinking, so each extra drink uses two empties net. Starting with `N` empties allows `N / 2` extra drinks. If two empties remain, borrowing one enables the final exchange, after which the borrowed bottle can be repaid.

The total is the original `N` drinks plus integer division `N / 2`. Read values until EOF and print one result per value.
