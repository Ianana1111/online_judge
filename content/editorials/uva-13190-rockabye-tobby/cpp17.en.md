`greater<pair<...>>` turns the priority queue into a minimum heap. Each pair's second field is the immutable input index, so tie priority remains stable across rescheduling. Names and periods are retrieved through the same ID.

Adding `period[id]` to the popped event continues that medicine's own arithmetic sequence. Pop then push keeps exactly N heap entries, while other simultaneous events remain available and are ordered by index. Every test case creates fresh vectors and a fresh heap.
