Use one bitset row per selected-person count; bit `sum` says whether that weight is reachable. C11 has no built-in large bitset, so each row uses 704 `uint64_t` words, covering sums 0 through 45000. To add a person of weight `w`, shift the previous-count row by `w`, splitting the shift into whole words and remaining bits and carrying across word boundaries. Update counts downward so that person is used only once.

Inspect every reachable bit in row `n/2`, including sums above half the total: with odd `n`, the smaller team by membership can be heavier. Minimize `|total-2*sum|`, then print the smaller team weight first.
