Check the two dividing lines first: if x equals the center x or y equals the center y, print `divisa` rather than forcing the point into a quadrant. Otherwise compare y to choose N or S, then x to choose E or O. The west abbreviation is O, not W. Each group supplies its query count and center, followed by exactly that many points. A zero query count ends input; zero coordinates are ordinary values.

The Python version splits whitespace-separated input and advances by each group’s actual field count.
