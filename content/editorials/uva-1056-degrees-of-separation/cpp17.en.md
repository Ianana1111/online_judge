The name-to-index lambda reuses existing IDs and assigns consecutive IDs only to new names. The distance matrix always has `n` rows and columns, so an unnamed person's row retains only its diagonal zero.

The `k` loop is outermost, matching the invariant that intermediate vertices are opened one at a time. `inf` is one million, far above the maximum finite path of 49, and even adding two infinite values remains within `int`.

The final scan covers all `n*n` entries. If its maximum remains infinity the specified word is printed; otherwise the numeric diameter is used. Each case recreates both the matrix and name map.
