The outer condition reads a pair and then checks `(a != 0 || b != 0)`, so only the pair `0 0` terminates input. `carry` and `operations` are reset for every pair.

Inside the digit loop, `% 10` extracts the two current digits. The expression assigned to `sum` includes the old carry before `carry` is overwritten by `sum >= 10`. Adding this new Boolean value to `operations` counts the carry generated by this column, rather than the carry arriving from the previous one.

Dividing both numbers by ten advances to the next column. The `a != 0 || b != 0` condition naturally handles numbers of different lengths. Finally, the output handles zero separately and selects a singular or plural suffix for a positive count, preserving the required phrases and punctuation.
