`survivor` resets to zero for every candidate, representing the sole remaining position at size one. The loop ends at `n-1` because region one has already been removed, and comparison with 11 maps original region 13 into this reduced zero-based list.

`step` begins at one and increments only after a full failed recurrence. Finding a match exits the search for that N, while the outer loop continues with the next input. Modulo keeps all intermediate positions bounded without any live-region array.
