Derive the answer before choosing code: constant acceleration makes velocity change linearly. Over the interval from 0 to `2t`, the average velocity is the velocity `v` at time `t`. Displacement is average velocity times duration, or `v * 2t`.

`Scanner.hasNextLong()` reads until EOF, including a `0 0` case. Read two `long` values and compute `2 * velocity * time`. Integer arithmetic is sufficient, and a negative velocity must remain negative in the result.
