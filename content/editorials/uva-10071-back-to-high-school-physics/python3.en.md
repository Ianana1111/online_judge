It is tempting to treat `v` as the initial velocity. Draw the interval from 0 to `2t` instead: the known velocity sits exactly in the middle. Under constant acceleration, velocity changes linearly, so this middle value is also the average velocity. Multiply it by the full duration to get `2 * v * t`.

The program reads until EOF and groups whitespace-separated numbers into pairs, so extra spaces or line breaks do not change the result. `0 0` is still a case, and negative displacement must keep its sign.
