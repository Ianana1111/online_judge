First check how input ends: `0 0` is valid data, so keep reading until EOF. `scanf` returns 2 only when it has read both numbers of a case.

The given velocity is measured halfway through the interval, not at its start. With constant acceleration, that velocity equals the average velocity over the full interval of length `2t`. The displacement is therefore `2 * velocity * time`. A negative velocity correctly produces a negative displacement without a special case.
