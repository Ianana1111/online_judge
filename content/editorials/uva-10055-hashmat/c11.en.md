Try `10 12` and `12 10` first: both answers must be 2. Subtracting in a fixed order is therefore unsafe. Compare the two values and subtract the smaller from the larger.

The limit includes `2^32`, which does not fit in a 32-bit `int` or `unsigned int`. Use `long long` for both values and the difference. `scanf` returning 2 means a full pair was read; `0 0` is a normal case, not a terminator.
