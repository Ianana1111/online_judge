Cross products classify the lines without slope division, including vertical lines. A zero cross product of direction vectors means parallel; checking the displacement between starting points against the first direction then distinguishes `LINE` from `NONE`.

For nonparallel lines, the intersection parameter `t` is a ratio of two integer cross products. Keep x and y as integer numerator/denominator pairs and round only at the final two-decimal formatting step. The C++ source includes only the standard headers it uses.
