`Fraction` parses the original coordinate strings. The least common multiple of their denominators supplies `scale`; multiplying coordinates and the travel limit by it turns all squared comparisons into exact integer arithmetic.

`adjacent[gopher]` lists reachable hole indices. `owner[hole]` is −1 for an empty hole or the index of its current gopher. `augment` marks a hole before following its owner, preventing cycles. The assignment changes only after the hole is empty or its owner has successfully moved elsewhere.

Every outer attempt receives a fresh `[False] * m`. Its Boolean return contributes zero or one to `saved`, and `n - saved` is the requested number left outside. The shared input iterator continues with another complete case until EOF.
