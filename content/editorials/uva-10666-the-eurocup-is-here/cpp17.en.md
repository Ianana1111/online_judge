Champion zero is handled before any lowest-bit operation. For nonzero X, `__builtin_popcountll(x)+1` counts all forced ancestors plus the team's own rank position.

With unsigned arithmetic, `x & (~x+1)` is the well-defined two's-complement expression that isolates the lowest set bit. `1ULL << n` constructs the total team count in the wide unsigned type.

Subtracting `block-1` forced descendants from the total yields the worst position. Each test prints the two bounds without constructing or storing the bracket.
