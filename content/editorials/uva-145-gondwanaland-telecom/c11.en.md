Convert both clock times to minutes since midnight. If finish is at or before start, add one day to finish; equal clock times then correctly mean 24 hours. Visit each minute in `[start, finish)`, reduce it modulo 1440, and place it in the day, evening, or night bucket. Midnight needs no separate branch.

Keep rates in cents and multiply bucket counts before formatting dollars with two decimal digits. The `printf` widths are 10 for the phone, 6 for each count, 3 for the plan, and 8 for the charge. Store the phone as text to preserve leading zeroes.
